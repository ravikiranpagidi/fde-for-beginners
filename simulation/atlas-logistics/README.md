# Atlas Logistics: begin the engagement

**Stage 1: customer brief and discovery task.** Complete [Northstar](../../project/northstar-support-copilot/README.md) first. This is a judgment simulation, not a second coding capstone. All organizations, people, numbers, payloads, and incidents are fictional.

> Atlas Logistics has a growing shipment-exception backlog. Operations leaders say employees spend too much time investigating delayed shipments across several systems. Leadership believes an AI agent could automate the process.

You meet an operations sponsor who wants a demonstration this month. They describe a queue of shipments marked "needs attention" and say experienced staff know which cases matter. You have not observed the workflow, confirmed data access, measured the backlog, or identified the authority to change shipment state. Treat these gaps as unknowns, not permission to invent a system.

## Working agreement

Keep one evidence folder, with dated revisions rather than overwriting initial assumptions.

## Discovery task

Produce **10 to 15 prioritized questions**. For each, state who can answer, the decision affected, and why it must be asked now or can wait. Rank your first five. A hundred generic questions will not establish a useful pilot boundary.

Also produce:

- A stakeholder map distinguishing users, sponsor, data/system owners, approval authority, and operator. Mark unconfirmed roles.
- A workflow map from backlog entry to investigation, decision, downstream action, and closure. Mark what you have observed versus inferred.
- Separate lists of facts, assumptions, and unknowns with an owner and verification method for each material unknown.
- A risk list with impact, likelihood rationale, containment option, and the first assumption to test.
- An initial success definition with a baseline collection method, denominator, quality guardrail, and decision owner. Do not turn the sponsor's desire for AI into a success metric.

Explain which question could cause you to recommend no build, and which access or authority question blocks implementation. Do not choose frameworks yet.

## Exit gate
