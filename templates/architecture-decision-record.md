# Architecture decision record

## When to use this

When alternatives create meaningful differences in risk, operation, cost, or user behavior. Do not write an ADR for every coding choice.

## Who contributes

Decision owner, implementer, affected dependency owners, and a reviewer able to challenge assumptions.

## What good looks like

A future engineer can understand why the choice was reasonable, what was rejected, and when to revisit it.
