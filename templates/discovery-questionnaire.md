# Discovery questionnaire

## When to use this

Before selecting a solution, and whenever observed workflow behavior contradicts the design.

## Who contributes

Actual users, sponsor, system/data owners, security reviewer, and the engineer responsible for delivery.

## What good looks like

A replayable workflow, prioritized unknowns, and evidence that changes a decision. Label observations, assumptions, and conflicting accounts separately.
