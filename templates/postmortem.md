# Postmortem

## When to use this

After an incident, near miss, or repeated failure reveals a weakness in the system or delivery process.

## Who contributes

Responders, operator, dependency owners, affected workflow representative, and a facilitator.

## What good looks like

An evidence-based account that explains conditions and decisions, improves the system, and assigns verifiable actions without blaming a person.
