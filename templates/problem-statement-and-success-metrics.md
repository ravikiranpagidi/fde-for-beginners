# Problem statement and success metrics

## When to use this

After initial discovery, before committing to scope or a launch date.

## Who contributes

Users, sponsor, delivery engineer, domain reviewer, and the owner of measurement data.

## What good looks like

A falsifiable outcome with a baseline, denominator, guardrails, and an accountable acceptance owner.
